The Betovi rule

The hobby is allowed to be irrational. The buying decision doesn’t have to be.

Measure first. “Fits 1:18” is not a measurement.

Check the object. A model engine can be a replica, a moving kit, or a science-class cousin wearing chrome.

Leave some air. Maximum capacity and good display capacity are not the same thing.

Commercial second. Sometimes the right recommendation is still “don’t buy another thing yet.”
