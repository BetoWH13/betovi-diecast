The Betovi rule

The hobby can be irrational. The setup doesn’t have to be.

Measure first. “Fits 1:18” is not a measurement.

Leave air. Maximum capacity is not display capacity.

Build one scene. More props do not automatically create more story.

Commercial second. Sometimes the correct purchase is still nothing.
