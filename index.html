Know what is actually on the shelf

Not every scale model is the same kind of object.

Collectors use “diecast” casually, but the hobby spans metal castings, resin display models, plastic parts, mixed construction and full build kits. Material is not a quality score by itself. It changes how a model is made, handled, repaired and enjoyed.

More interested in building than collecting? Start with advanced kits →
Diecast metal

Often chosen for the familiar weight and feel of a metal-bodied model. Construction and detail still vary enormously by maker and price tier.

Resin

Common in display-focused models and smaller production niches. Judge the actual model, finish and construction — not the material label alone.

Plastic & mixed construction

Interiors, glazing, trim, chassis pieces and detailed components regularly mix materials. “Metal” rarely means every part is metal.

Build kits

The object arrives unfinished because the making is part of the hobby. Difficulty, tools, workspace and patience matter as much as the subject.
