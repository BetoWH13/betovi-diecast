Know what is actually on the shelf

“Scale model” is not one kind of object.

Finished metal models, resin pieces, mixed construction and build kits can all belong in the same collection, but they reward different expectations.

Diecast metal

Often chosen for familiar weight, opening-feature traditions, and the feel of a finished collectible. Construction varies widely by maker and price tier.

Resin

Common in display-focused models and smaller production niches. Often favors exterior form and finish over moving or opening features.

Plastic & mixed construction

Interiors, glazing, trim, chassis pieces and detail parts may use several materials even when a model is casually called “diecast.”

Build kits

The object arrives unfinished because the making is part of the hobby. Difficulty, tools, workspace and patience matter as much as subject choice.
